# Playtest — Phase 1 (Gate 1)

Goal: find out if the mechanic is fun. **Gate 1 passes if at least 3 testers out of 5 start a new game on their own.**

## Setup

- Build: debug APK of the prototype, installed on your phone. Note the commit hash: `________`
- Balancing used (from `data/balancing.tres`): rows `__`, distorted reflection `on / off`, period `__`.
- Phone fully charged, sound off, no notifications.
- 5 testers who have never seen the game. One tester at a time.

## Protocol

1. Hand over the phone with the game open on a new board. Say only: "It's a puzzle game prototype, play as you like. I'm just watching."
2. **Do not explain anything.** Do not answer questions during play; note them instead ("What do you think it does?" is the only allowed reply).
3. Watch in silence. Note the time of each game start and end.
4. When a game ends, say nothing. Note if the tester taps Restart on their own.
5. Stop after 10 minutes, or when the tester hands the phone back.
6. Then ask the debrief questions.

## Debrief questions

1. In your own words, what are the rules?
2. What did the right side do? When did you understand it?
3. Was anything confusing or frustrating?
4. Would you play again later? Why?
5. Anything else?
